01 / The decision

Approve a two-week trial of scheduled development clusters in September. Engineering can recover an estimated $2,100 per month without changing production capacity. Keep commitment purchases on hold until the seasonal traffic review.

Guardrail: preserve the 99.9% availability objective and p95 job latency below 45 seconds. Stop the trial if either measure deteriorates.

03 / Efficiency, with context

Completed jobs increased from 1.00 million to 1.12 million. Dividing total spend by completed jobs gives $0.090 in July and $0.0752 in August. The 16.4% improvement is directional: job complexity and customer mix also changed.

Availability was 99.96% and p95 latency was 38 seconds in August, compared with 99.95% and 39 seconds in July. These observations support continuing the trial; they do not establish causation.

05 / Forecast and watchpoints

September planning assumes the current workload mix and no new commitments. A $83,000–$88,000 range covers the expected traffic variation; it is a planning scenario, not a statistical confidence interval. Trial savings are excluded until measured.

Watch cross-region transfer during the recovery exercise and database storage after the retention change. Each should be reviewed against its operational purpose before reducing spend.
